Complete Cardputer started as an idea on paper and gradually became a working prototype. It is an attempt to bring a collection of useful features and experiments together in one place for the M5Stack Cardputer ADV.

The current version is an experimental, AI-assisted project made for learning and fun. It is not a professional product, and I do not present it as professional work on LinkedIn; the point is to explore the device, test ideas and learn by building.

The long-term plan is to understand every part of the firmware more deeply and, as the project matures, rewrite the core in C++. For now, it remains an open-ended maker project that documents the process rather than pretending to be finished.
