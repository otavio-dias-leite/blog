My interest in technology started in 2018, when I built a small RFID project after purchasing my first kit. I spent time understanding how the signals worked, experimenting with hexadecimal codes and exploring what I could do with the technology. It was a fun experience that eventually led me to hacking courses, the Cardputer ADV and, later, a degree in Computer Science. Each of these experiences gave me another reason to keep exploring this field.

Building a career in embedded systems and information security is not necessarily easy, especially in my region of Brazil, where opportunities and specialized communities can be limited. Sometimes I question whether the lack of progress comes from the local job market, limited resources or even my own lack of dedication. I have achieved good academic results and passed competitive selection processes, but I know that potential alone does not guarantee a successful career. For now, much of this exploration remains a hobby. One example is Netrunner, a firmware project I developed with the assistance of AI, which has given me an opportunity to experiment and learn.

Looking ahead, I intend to pursue postgraduate studies in Embedded Systems for IoT and Information Security. I do not know exactly where this path will take me, or how difficult it will be to turn these interests into a profession, but I intend to keep learning, building projects and exploring both fields. Perhaps, eventually, what started as curiosity in 2018 will become my career.
