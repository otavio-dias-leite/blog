I put together **COMPLETE**, an experimental firmware for the **M5Stack Cardputer ADV**. The idea was to bring several functions together in one place, with a built-in launcher and features for managing and running applications, exploring media, connectivity, games, and other tools. The project is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/otavio-dias-leite/complete-cardputer), and its [project website](https://otavio-dias-leite.github.io/complete-cardputer/) includes the firmware flasher and catalog.

This is an AI-assisted project: I used AI to help turn the idea into a working experiment and build out its features. It is not professional software, a commercial product, or a claim of expertise. I made it for fun, to try things on the device and see what I could put together.

I have not added COMPLETE to my LinkedIn, because I do not consider it a professional project or a portfolio achievement. It is just a silly little hobby project — something I wanted to make, use, and share with anyone else who might find it interesting.
