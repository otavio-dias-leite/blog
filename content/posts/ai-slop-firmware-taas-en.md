I've been developing a few firmwares for the M5Stack Cardputer ADV, including Johnny, Chryse and Cyberspace. They're all still in Alpha. I use AI to turn ideas into something functional, while manually fixing code, testing on hardware and making adjustments. Basically, AI Slop and my own TAAS (*Toy as a Service*, lol).

The cyberpunk aesthetics and hacker themes are just the playthings of a technology enthusiast. I'm not trying to portray myself as some super hacker or genius developer. I've written code manually at a federal university and scored above 9/10 in my courses, but I'm not claiming to be a software specialist. I share these projects for my own use, with friends and anyone interested, just as I've enjoyed Minecraft mods and even entire games made with AI assistance.

In the future, I do intend to develop firmware more independently, working closer to the hardware and with low-level programming, both for the Cardputer and conventional computers. For now, these are just technological toys to experiment with, learn from and have fun building.
