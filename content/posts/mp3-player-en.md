**Johnny** is a music player firmware project for the **M5Stack Cardputer ADV**, inspired by **Johnny Silverhand**, the rebellious rocker from *Cyberpunk 2077*. The name is a nod to the character's loud, defiant attitude and the idea of carrying your own music everywhere.

The project turns the Cardputer ADV into a compact, standalone music player. Instead of relying on a phone or an internet connection, Johnny plays local music directly from a microSD card, with a dedicated interface designed for the device.

## Music playback

Johnny is built around the experience of listening to music on a small, portable piece of hardware. Its features include:

- Browse music files and folders stored on a microSD card.
- Play, pause, stop, skip tracks and seek through a song.
- Read ID3 metadata, including track titles and artists.
- Display embedded album artwork when supported.
- Five-band equalizer, playback modes and audio visualizers.
- Multiple color themes and integration with the Cardputer's RGB LED.

The firmware is developed for the constraints of the ESP32-S3, with incremental audio decoding and buffering intended to keep playback and the interface responsive.

## A personal hardware project

Johnny is also an ongoing experiment in embedded development: exploring audio playback, storage, user interfaces and the limits of the Cardputer ADV. It is a personal project made out of curiosity and a love of music, hardware and the Cyberpunk universe.
