I decided to focus my time, studies, and career on one specific field: embedded systems. It is an area I already have experience with and genuinely enjoy.

Right now, I’m working toward building a career in the field by pursuing certifications, taking courses, and looking for job opportunities. It also fits well with my previous education and gives me a chance to make good use of what I’ve already learned.

Embedded systems have actually been a hobby of mine for a long time. I’m constantly learning about the field, following new projects, and buying and experimenting with hardware. I currently have an Arduino, ESP-01, ESP8266, ESP32-S3, Cardputer ADV, Raspberry Pi Zero W, Raspberry Pi 5, 4, 3, 2, Pico, and several other microcontrollers. I know how to program and have used these devices for different projects and purposes. I’ve also worked with a wide range of electronic components, soldering equipment, power supplies, and other tools.

I also cleaned up my GitHub and removed projects that no longer represent the direction I want to take, such as games and general programming exercises.

From this point forward, I want to build my portfolio and career around embedded systems and become a specialist in the field.
