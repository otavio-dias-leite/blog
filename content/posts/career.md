My interest in technology began in childhood, driven by curiosity about electronics, computers, and how things work. At 13, I received an RFID kit from my father, which sparked an early interest in access control and security. At 14, I placed first in the entrance exam for the Electrotechnics program at IFF. At 17, I was admitted to Electrical Engineering, and at 20, I began a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science. Alongside my formal education, I have completed independent projects involving electrical installations, electronics, and physical systems.

I am currently expanding my knowledge of Information Security, a field closely connected to my Computer Science studies. I have been taking courses in the area and working toward professional certifications, including CCNA, OSCP, CEH, and others. My interest has increasingly turned toward hardware hacking: I have always been curious about security, and after consuming more technical content about hacking, I felt strongly motivated to explore how devices, protocols, and systems work, how they can be analyzed, and how their security can be tested responsibly.

Embedded systems are a personal hobby, not the professional market I am currently pursuing. I take part in open-source and academic projects, acquire and experiment with different hardware products, and have recently started sharing this interest on my Instagram profile. I am open to both full-time employment (CLT) and freelance work in areas related to my education and background, including Electrotechnics, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, Information Security, and other roles where my qualifications can be applied.
