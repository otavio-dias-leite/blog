My interest in technology began in childhood, when I enjoyed experimenting with electronics, computers, and small game projects. At 12, my father and uncle gave me a Raspberry Pi and an Arduino, which sparked years of hands-on experimentation. At 14, I placed first in the entrance exam for the Electrotechnics program at IFF. At 17, I was admitted to Electrical Engineering, and at 20, I began a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science. Over the years, I have also completed several independent, informal projects involving electrical installations, electronics, and physical systems.

Electrical Engineering and Electrotechnics are my main professional fields. My experience and interests are centered on electrical and electronic systems, practical troubleshooting, installations, monitoring, and control. I am also studying Computer Science, which complements this background. Embedded systems remain an area I genuinely enjoy exploring, but they are a personal hobby rather than my main professional focus. Programming is another interest, not the career field I am primarily pursuing.

I am currently open to employment opportunities across my demonstrated competencies, especially in Electrical Engineering, Electrotechnics, and related technical roles. I am interested in applying my practical experience, technical education, and willingness to learn in professional environments, whether in electrical work, electronics, maintenance, technical support, or other areas aligned with my skills.
