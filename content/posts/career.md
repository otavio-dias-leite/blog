My academic background combines **Electrotechnics, Electrical Engineering, and Computer Science**. I began studying Electrotechnics at 14 and started Electrical Engineering at 17. My second degree is in **Computer Science**, not Information Systems.

My strongest foundation is understanding hardware and the physical principles behind it: electronic components, electrical systems, appliances, digital electronics, and computing infrastructure. I am also familiar with the broader foundations of computing, including networks, databases, and how systems fit together.

Programming has always been a hobby, and I have taken courses in cybersecurity, web development, and game development. I build personal projects with high-level tools, engines, and AI-assisted workflows. I have studied C, C++, and digital electronics, and continue to explore programming through curiosity and practical projects.

My interests are grounded in electrotechnics, electronics, electrical systems, and the foundations of computing. I focus on documenting knowledge and practical work that I can demonstrate, rather than presenting future interests as established professional skills.

I am open to **jobs, internships, freelance work, and collaborations** related to electrotechnics, electronics, technical support, and computing infrastructure. This blog documents what I study, build, and learn along the way.

As a personal note, I chose to pursue **electrical work rather than programming** as my main professional path. Programming remains a useful hobby and complementary skill, but it is not the center of my career.
