My interest in technology started long before university. As a child, I experimented with computers and game development, building small projects with Unity 3D and Scratch. At 12, my father and uncle gave me a Raspberry Pi and an Arduino, which led to years of hands-on experiments with electronics and physical devices. At 14, I placed first in the entrance exam for the Electrotechnics program at IFF. At 17, I was admitted to Electrical Engineering; I have not verified my exact ranking, but believe I placed among the top four. At 20, I began a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science at a private university. Along the way, I contributed to formal projects involving embedded systems and physical devices, and also carried out electrical installation work independently.

My professional foundation is in Electrical Engineering and Electrotechnics. I am interested in opportunities in Industry 4.0, especially where electrical systems, embedded devices, and IoT meet. This includes autonomous systems, off-grid solar installations, monitoring, and control. Programming began as a hobby, but studying Computer Science led me to approach it more seriously. I completed my first semester with a GPA above 9.2. I am also interested in Information Security, particularly protecting networks, connected devices, and electronic systems. My interest in security grew from hands-on experiments with an Arduino RFID kit my father gave me and from building an infrared signal receiver and transmitter. These projects sparked my curiosity about access control, communications, and device security.

I have taken part in cybersecurity training initiatives, including Hackers do Bem, and plan to strengthen my foundations through further study in areas such as CCNA, OSCP, and CEH. I am also interested in courses and certifications from providers such as Solyd and DESEC as complementary learning opportunities; I do not see certificates as a substitute for practical knowledge. My goal is to build a career connecting electrical and physical systems with secure, connected infrastructure, especially embedded devices, industrial IoT, and network security. I am open to professional opportunities, internships, and collaborations in these fields.
